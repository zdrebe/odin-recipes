Cherry Compote

Description

This easy to make compote makes a great accompaniement to vanilla ice cream. How can something so simple feel so luxurious!?

The additional spices can be adapted to personal taste. Personally, I love the way the star anise compliments the cherry flavour.

Ingredients

  • Cherries
  • A sweetener of choice (brown sugar or honey work well)
  • Star anise
  • Cinnamon

Steps

  1. Prep the cherries: Using a knife, pit the cherries by making an incision lengthways on each fruit. I recommend doing this over the saucepan we'll use to cook the fruit so as to catch all the delicious juice and save on mess.
  2. Add the other ingredients:This is a matter of taste. I add a little sugar or honey, 2-3 whole star anise and a small stick of cinnamon.
  3. Cook down: On a low-med heat, cook down the cherries, stirring occasionally as the fruit breaks down and takes on the flavours of the spices.
  4. Serve: Remove the whole spices and either serve immediately with vanilla ice cream or wait for it to cool and use a syou wish.
