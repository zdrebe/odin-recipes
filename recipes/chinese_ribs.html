Chinese Style Ribs

Description

A deceptively simple dish with bags of flavour. With a few Chinese pantry staples, some good ribs, and a couple of hours, you can create a crowd-pleasing meal with minimal effort!

Ingredients

  • 680g pork spare ribs
  • 1 tablespoon granulated white sugar
  • 2 tablespoons light soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon dark soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon Shaoxing cooking wine
  • 1 tablespoon rice vinegar
  • 237ml water
  • 1 tablespoon minced garlic
  • 1 tablespoon minced ginger
  • 1 green onion

Steps

  1. Clean the pork ribs: Cut the rips lengthways, add to a pot, add water, and bring to a boil. A scum will form on the top. Drain into a colander and rinse well
  2. Season: Wash the pot thoroughly and return it to the stove. Add the parboiled ribs, sugar, soy sauce, dark soy sauce, cooking wine, rice vinegar, water, ginger, and garlic.
  3. Simmer: Cover and simmer for 35 minutes, tossing halfway through for even cooking.
  4. Thicken Sauce: Remove lid and continue to cook until the sauce has thickened and coats ribs.
  5. Serve: Garnish with thinly sliced green onions and serve with rice.
